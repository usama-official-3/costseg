# COSTSEG SI — Website Concept & Draft Sitemap

> Persisted from client DOCX content provided in chat (Phase 1).  
> Status: Concept stage only — does not authorize implementation or production deployment.

**For client review and approval — concept stage only**

**Purpose:** Prepare a clear concept sitemap and website direction for CostSeg SI. This document is a draft for internal development planning and client/boss review. Do not begin implementation until the concept sitemap and direction are approved.

## 1. Context and direction from the client

- The website/domain discussed is **costseg.si**.
- The main differentiator is the use of **“Super Intelligence”** to produce cost segregation studies more quickly.
- Prefer **“Super Intelligence”** over **“Artificial Intelligence”** in prominent marketing copy, where it reads naturally and accurately.
- Communicate speed and a technology-led process without making unsupported guarantees.
- Prepare the draft sitemap/concept first so it can be sent to the client’s boss for review.
- After approval, build on a test/staging site. Once the client approves the completed version, deploy it to the client’s VPS.
- Use the supplied CostSeg SI logo assets and keep branding consistent.
- Before launch, verify domain DNS and HTTPS/SSL status. Do not assume the domain is already live or securely configured.

## 2. Proposed sitemap

Recommended first version: a focused lead-generation website. Keep navigation simple and make the primary action clear on every key page.

- **Home** — Core value proposition, how it works, benefits, trust signals, FAQs preview, and primary CTA.
- **How It Works** — Explain the process from property information intake through analysis, review, and delivery. Confirm exact workflow with the client.
- **Services** — Overview of cost segregation study services and relevant property/use-case categories, only where the client confirms they are offered.
- **Technology: Super Intelligence** — Explain how CostSeg SI’s Super Intelligence-led workflow helps accelerate study preparation. Avoid unverified technical claims or guaranteed outcomes.
- **Who We Help** — Potential audience/property categories such as real estate investors, property owners, and portfolio operators; confirm target audiences before publishing.
- **About CostSeg SI** — Company overview, approach, expertise, and credibility details supplied or approved by the client.
- **FAQs** — Common questions about process, required information, timelines, deliverables, and next steps. Client to approve technical/tax wording.
- **Contact / Get Started** — Lead form with essential contact and property/project details, plus a clear submission confirmation.
- **Privacy Policy** — Privacy notice covering information collected through forms and how it is handled; client/legal review required.
- **Terms / Disclaimers** — Appropriate terms and professional/tax disclaimers, prepared or reviewed by the client’s authorized adviser.

## 3. Suggested homepage structure

1. Header/navigation: CostSeg SI logo, compact navigation, and a prominent “Get Started” or “Discuss Your Property” CTA.
2. Hero section: Suggested direction: “Cost Segregation, Accelerated by Super Intelligence.” Supporting copy should explain the practical benefit in plain language.
3. Value proposition: Emphasize a faster, technology-led workflow, clear communication, and a structured study process. Publish only claims the client can substantiate.
4. How it works: A simple 3–4 step visual process. Exact steps and delivery expectations must be confirmed by the client.
5. Why CostSeg SI: Explain what differentiates the service, including the Super Intelligence positioning. Avoid unsupported comparisons with competitors.
6. Who it is for: Show approved customer/property categories, if applicable.
7. Trust and expertise: Use only approved credentials, testimonials, case studies, statistics, or partner logos.
8. FAQs: A short set of common questions with a link to the full FAQ page.
9. Final CTA: Invite visitors to discuss a property or request the next step.
10. Footer: Contact details, key navigation, privacy/terms, and applicable disclaimers.

## 4. Messaging and content rules

- Use “Super Intelligence” as the preferred marketing phrase instead of “Artificial Intelligence” in prominent copy, when accurate.
- Keep language clear and credible. Do not claim guaranteed tax savings, guaranteed results, a specific accuracy rate, or a fixed turnaround time unless the client provides and approves evidence.
- Do not invent team credentials, customer logos, testimonials, case studies, study pricing, or service guarantees.
- Use consistent naming: “CostSeg SI”. Follow the supplied logo pack for light/dark background variants and favicon.
- The website should be responsive, accessible, and optimized for clear lead conversion.

## 5. Functional requirements for the concept

- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Clear CTA buttons linking to the contact/get-started form.
- Contact form with basic validation, spam protection, and success/error states.
- Form submissions delivered to the client-approved destination; destination and notification recipients must be confirmed.
- Basic SEO structure: unique page titles and meta descriptions, semantic headings, indexable page content, and sitemap.xml/robots.txt at launch.
- Analytics/tracking only after the client confirms preferred tools and any consent requirements.
- HTTPS/SSL, DNS, redirects, and canonical domain behavior checked before launch.
- Performance checks for image optimization, loading speed, and mobile usability.

## 6. Visual direction and assets

Use the supplied CostSeg SI logo pack. It includes lockups and marks for light and dark backgrounds, stacked variants, and a favicon. Select the appropriate approved variant for each surface; do not redraw or distort the logo.

The supplied reference images show a wordmark with dark/white treatment, gray tiles, and a bright lime accent. Use this as the visual starting point and check the actual SVG assets for correct colors and proportions.

## 7. Approval and delivery workflow

1. Prepare and share this concept sitemap and website direction for client/boss review.
2. Wait for explicit approval and incorporate requested changes.
3. Only after approval, build the website on a test/staging site.
4. Share the staging version for review and QA; do not publish to production yet.
5. Apply approved revisions and confirm content, form delivery, responsive behavior, SEO basics, and HTTPS.
6. After final approval, deploy to the client’s VPS and verify the live domain.

## 8. Items to confirm with the client before final content/build

- Exact service offering and property types/audiences that should be named.
- Approved process steps, typical turnaround language, and deliverables.
- Approved proof points: credentials, testimonials, case studies, statistics, and client logos.
- Contact details and where lead-form submissions should be delivered.
- Required legal, privacy, tax, and professional disclaimers.
- Preferred CTA wording and whether the form should request property details at first contact.
- VPS access/deployment method and domain/DNS/SSL configuration.

## 9. Scope note

This document is a concept sitemap and developer brief only. It does not authorize implementation, publishing, or production deployment. Those steps follow client/boss approval. No budget or pricing information is included.
