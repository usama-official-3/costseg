# CONTENT_AND_RESEARCH_STANDARDS — CostSeg SI

## Purpose

Standards for any educational or research content on costseg.si. Prevents publishing unverified tax material or inventing authority.

## Content type distinctions (required)

Every research page must make clear which category content belongs to:

1. **Primary legal authority** — statutes, regulations, court opinions
2. **Official IRS / government resources** — pubs, forms, ATG, notices
3. **Secondary commentary** — articles, analyses (clearly labeled)
4. **Company educational explanations** — CostSeg SI plain-language teaching
5. **Hypothetical / illustrative examples** — labeled as illustrative; list assumptions

## Citation and sourcing

- Cite statutes, regulations, IRS materials, and court opinions
- Link to original sources wherever possible
- Record relevant dates, tax years, effective dates, and last review date
- Maintain an update process for law changes (`Tax Law Update Archive` proposed in Group H)

## Court cases (Group D)

The draft case list is a **research shortlist**, not a verified set of cost-segregation precedents.

Before publication of any case page, verify:

- Citation
- Court and date
- Holding
- Relevance to cost segregation (some items may only address general depreciation)

Required case-page fields (when verified): court, date, citation, facts, issue, holding, reasoning, relevance, source opinion link, review status.

## Asset classification (Group F)

Classifications depend on facts, function, applicable law, and evidence. Avoid categorical tax conclusions without qualified review.

## Case studies (Group G)

- Use sourced real cases only when verified
- Clearly label hypothetical scenarios
- State all assumptions

## Messaging prohibitions

Do **not**:

- Invent legal cases, client results, testimonials, credentials, or savings statistics
- Promise guaranteed tax savings, guaranteed IRS compliance, audit-proof results, or fixed turnaround times without approved evidence
- Present educational material as individualized tax advice
- Claim unverified Super Intelligence agents are operational as a live product

## Calculator (if ever approved)

Requires documented assumptions, qualified review, clear limitations, and disclaimers before release. Frontend preview only until then.

## Publication gate

Qualified tax/legal review required before publishing Groups C–G content beyond clearly labeled placeholders/samples.
